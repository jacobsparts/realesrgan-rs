Model description from OpenModelDB:

This model was made to upscale realistic low-res textures that are compressed
by either JPEG or BC1. It was tested on realistic GameCube textures and may
also work on real-life images, especially outdoor images.

The converted safetensors file is a format conversion of
4x_RealisticRescaler_100000_G.pth; the tensor values are unchanged.
